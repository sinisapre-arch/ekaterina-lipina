01 — Concept
The Idea

Luxury through restraint

The reference is not a CV. It is the front of a magazine — a masthead that commands the page, imagery that carries the weight, and typography doing the rest. Burgundy never shouts; it appears as a hairline, a folio number, a single italicised word.

01

The page is the product

Full-bleed photography, oversized type, generous margins. Every viewport is composed like a spread, not a scroll of sections.

02

One accent, used sparingly

Burgundy is reserved for emphasis: rules, folio numbers, hover states, the italicised word in a headline. Never a background wash, never a button unless it is the only call to action.

03

Two languages, one voice

EN and RU share the same grid and the same typefaces. Cyrillic sits in Playfair Display and Golos Text natively — and the name itself switches script, Екатерина Сосноватая, so the Russian edition is typographically equal, not a translation crammed in afterwards.

02 — Palette
Colour

Ink, paper, burgundy

The deep burgundy is sampled directly from the original studio backdrop, so the interface and the images share one exact colour instead of approximately matching. The photography itself is graded to a single monochrome with burgundy in the shadows — the images sit inside the palette rather than fighting it.

03 — Typography
Type scale

Didone display, quiet support

Four families, all self-hosted with Cyrillic subsets. Playfair Display carries the voice; Golos Text stays out of the way; JetBrains Mono handles the magazine furniture — folios, labels, metadata.

04 — Grid & rhythm
Structure

Twelve columns, deliberately broken

A 12-column field keeps the page honest, but the composition rarely fills it. Images step in and out of the grid, captions hang in the margin, and the eye is pulled across a spread rather than down a list.

07 — Motion
Behaviour

Slow reveals, no bounce

Images settle from a slight over-scale and dim into place. Text rises a short distance, masked. Nothing overshoots, nothing bounces — the motion reads as a camera settling, not a UI animating.

01 · Enter — 1.14× / 35%
02 · Settle — 1.07× / 70%
03 · Rest — 1.00× / 100%
Timing. Image reveal 1100ms cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1). Text rise 900ms, 60ms stagger between lines. Hover 300ms. All motion respects prefers-reduced-motion — frames settle instantly and nothing moves.
